Jason X. Liu

I am currently a Postdoctoral Research Scholar in Chemical Engineering at Stanford University supported by an NIH T32 Award, and my current research focuses on protein language modeling and reinforcement learning. I am currently advised by Profs. Grant Rotskoff and Alex Dunn.

Previously, I completed my PhD and MA in Mechanical Engineering at Princeton University as a Gordon Y.S. Wu Fellow, where my research focused on soft matter physics. My advisors were Profs. Rodney Priestley and Craig Arnold.

Before that, I completed my BA in Physics at UC Berkeley, during which I was a student researcher at Lawrence Livermore National Lab. I was advised by Dr. Andrea Schmidt.

I am very grateful for the tremendous support of all my past and current advisors.
